DE Biology 101 — Resources by Unit

A dual-enrollment general biology course runs on a college syllabus inside a high school calendar, and the pieces a student needs sit in three different places. This page puts the reading, the interactive tools and the shape of each unit in one order: the eighteen units of NVCC BIO 101, with a free open-textbook chapter, the calendar window, the laboratory that falls inside it, and the Checkpoint that goes with it. Everything here is free and opens on a phone or an iPad.

Three places, and what lives in each

  • Battlefield Canvas — slides, readings, the lab documents and the shared class data table. Course files are there, not here.
  • NOVA Canvas — the Checkpoint questions, all of them, all year, and where a finished lab is uploaded from Lab 2 onward.
  • This page — the reading and the interactive tools, arranged in the order the course meets them.

The Battlefield grade and the NOVA grade are the same grade: one piece of work, scored once, recorded in both.

And one more thing, which is yours rather than any of the three: Student-Authored Modules — what they are, and why you build your own.
