Biology Classroom Tools

Interactive activities I build for my ninth-grade Biology I and Advanced Biology I classes, filed by unit so any Virginia teacher can find the one that fits tomorrow’s lesson. Each runs in Safari on a school iPad with no login and no printing, and each is mapped to the Virginia Biology SOL and to the Prince William County Public Schools pacing guide. Units and block counts follow the PWCS Biology I pacing guide for 2026–27. Each tool keeps its Bio Tool number for good; numbers run in the order the tools were built, and a tool that serves two units appears in both.

Unit 1 Biochemistry

PWCS pacing guide · Quarter 1 · 11 blocks

Virginia SOL for this unit
  • BIO.2a water chemistry has an influence on life processes
  • BIO.2b macromolecules have roles in maintaining life processes
  • BIO.2c enzymes have a role in biochemical processes
  • Bio Tool #2 Biochemistry Vocabulary Crosswords — three puzzles, one for each learning target: water, macromolecules, and enzymes Interactive · works on iPads · checks its own letters · about 40 minutes for all three · SOL BIO.2a, 2b, 2c · PWCS LT 1.1, 1.2, 1.3
  • Bio Tool #10 The Water Patch — drag water molecules until hydrogen bonds snap into place, then freeze, boil, stir and salt them; a second module folds a protein in water and runs an enzyme through induced fit and catalysis Interactive · works on iPads · nothing submitted · two modules, nine steps, about 45 minutes · set the toggle to 9th Grade SOL Bio · SOL BIO.2a, 2c · PWCS LT 1.1, 1.3
  • Bio Tool #17 The Macromolecule Patch — snap sugars, amino acids and nucleotides into chains and count the water that leaves at every link, split them again with the right enzyme, take an egg apart, sort molecules by job, read a molecule from its parts and test real foods with indicator drops Interactive · works on iPads · nothing submitted · six steps, about 30 minutes · set the toggle to 9th Grade SOL Bio · SOL BIO.2b · PWCS LT 1.2

Unit 2 Cell Structure & Function

PWCS pacing guide · Quarter 1 · 8 blocks

Virginia SOL for this unit

  • Bio Tool #8 SOL Practice, Unit 2 — thirty original practice questions on cell theory, cell types and organelles, homeostasis, the membrane and transport, filed by learning target, each answered with the reasoning and the standard it was written from Interactive · works on iPads · nothing submitted · multiple choice, select-all, ordering and matching · projector warm-up mode · SOL BIO.3a, 3b, 3d, 2a · PWCS LT 2.1–2.5
  • Bio Tool #5 The Membrane Patch — build a piece of plasma membrane one molecule at a time, turn it in three dimensions, and run its aquaporin, its Na⁺/K⁺ pump and its receptor Interactive 3D · works on iPads · nothing submitted · nine steps, about 25 minutes · set the toggle to Biology I · SOL BIO.3d, 3b, 2b · PWCS Module 2
  • Bio Tool #6 Four Classes, One Membrane — a tappable cross-section of the membrane with a class filter, animated passive and active transport, and a tonicity beaker Interactive · works on iPads · nothing submitted · about 15 minutes · five-question quick check · SOL BIO.3d
  • Bio Tool #7 The Membrane, Built From All Four — the same cross-section drawn plainly, part by part, with an eight-question self-check on transport, tonicity, the fluid mosaic and junctions Interactive · works on iPads · nothing submitted · about 15 minutes · eight-question self-check · SOL BIO.3d
  • Bio Tool #13 Cell Check — fifteen questions across the five Module 2 learning targets, from what makes something a theory to how a cell moves material across its membrane, each with the reasoning and something to draw Interactive · works on iPads · nothing submitted · about 12 minutes · LT 2.1–2.5 · set the tab to Biology I · SOL BIO.3a, 3b, 3d · PWCS Module 2
  • Bio Tool #18 The Protein Route — follow one insulin molecule out of a pancreas cell: the copy leaves the nucleus, a ribosome links the chain on the rough ER, the Golgi sorts four proteins to four places, blood sugar opens the packets, then block a stop and see what piles up Interactive · works on iPads · nothing submitted · seven steps, about 20 minutes · set the switch to SOL Bio · LT 2.2 and 2.5 · SOL BIO.3b · PWCS Module 2, Lesson 2 and Lesson 6
  • Bio Tool #19 The Cancer Pathway — one skin cell stops listening to its neighbors; watch its descendants pile into a tumor, pull in a blood supply, break through the layer under the tissue, ride the bloodstream and start a second tumor in the lung Animation · watch only, nothing to answer · about a minute and a half on a loop · Projector mode for the front of the room · LT 2.3 · SOL BIO.3 · PWCS Module 2, cancer project launch
  • Bio Tool #21 Cell Structure and Function Vocabulary Crosswords — seven puzzles across the five learning targets: cell theory and how science knows, two kinds of cells, organelles working together, one cell or many and homeostasis, the membrane, and passive and active transport Interactive · works on iPads · checks its own letters · 78 terms, about 10 minutes a puzzle · SOL BIO.3a, 3b, 3d · PWCS LT 2.1–2.5

Using these with Canvas

The tools send nothing back to the teacher, so collect the work with a screenshot uploaded to a Canvas assignment. The steps, what to do about answer keys, and what to do when students share iPads are in A Note to Teachers Using the Classroom Tools, which is updated as new tools arrive.
