Blue Ridge Salamander Project

Turn over a rock in the Blue Ridge, find the animal's DNA, and decode it gene by gene. The animal stays hidden until every gene checks correct.

  1. Start hereTurn the Rock and DecodeRoll for a site, turn a rock, roll for the lab slip, get a Creature Code. Check each gene you decode on paper.
  2. With your codeFind the GenesFifteen pairs of chromosomes. Highlight the TATA box, the start, the stop and the gene on each strip.
  3. Whole classCodon BingoOne caller, a different card on every iPad, and a round played on your own animal.
  4. PreviewThe Animal in 3DFour sample animals at night, with sound. A device test.
