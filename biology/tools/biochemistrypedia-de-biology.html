Bio Tool #31 DE Biology 101: A Biochemistrypedia Resource Guide by Unit

Biochemistrypedia is a free biology site from Prof. Christopher Deppmann’s lab at the University of Virginia. Its college courses in Biochemistry, Cell Biology, Genetics, Cell Signaling and Ecology and Evolution were written for biology majors and premedical students, so they run deeper than Biology 101. The lessons that fit are filed under the eighteen units of the course, with the high school version of the same idea beside each unit for a student who needs the ground floor first.

How to read a college lesson in a dual-enrollment course

  • Every college lesson has Explain at my level, with High school, College and Graduate settings. Start at High school, read it, then switch to College.
  • Each lesson has a short film, simulations, real molecules from the Protein Data Bank to turn in 3D, self-test questions with each answer explained, and a few original MCAT-style questions. The MCAT-style questions are a good test of whether a Checkpoint idea has really landed.
  • They are longer and harder than the chapter. Read the section that answers your Checkpoint miss, not the whole lesson.
  • The films use an AI narrator voice, and some slides were drawn with AI help; the site names who checked each one. Mistakes go to the authors through the error form.
  • Open one on a school iPad first; the site is new and a filter may not know it yet.

How the labels read

  • Teach with ita lesson that covers the unit's standard at the level the unit is taught
  • Fills a gapcovers something students find hard, or something this site has no tool for yet
  • Review and Flexgood for reassessment, Flex remediation and spaced review before the test
  • One step downthe same idea at an easier reading level, for a student who needs the words first
  • One step upthe college version of the idea, for a student ready to go further
  • Stretchdeeper than the course asks, for a student who wants more
  • Teacher referencebackground for planning, not a student assignment

Unit 1 Course Overview; Introduction to Biology

Quarter 1 · Aug 24–Aug 28 · Checkpoint Chapter 1

An opening unit about what biology studies and how a claim gets tested. Two college lessons carry that second idea well, and a third gives the whole course in one sitting.

  • Teach with itIntroduction to Biochemistry and Cell Biology: Compartments Create ControlWhy a few kinds of atoms build all of life, the four classes of molecules, and the central dogma, with a cell-size scaler that shows why cells stay small. A preview of the whole semester.Biochemistry · about 22 min · short film, 3 simulations, 3D molecules, 5 self-test questions
  • Review and FlexVitalism: How Chemistry Captured LifeThe century-long argument over whether living chemistry follows its own laws, from Wöhler’s urea to Buchner’s cell-free fermentation. A good story about how evidence settles a question.Biochemistry · about 18 min · short film, 2 simulations, 3D molecules, 4 self-test questions
  • StretchExperiments That Distinguish MechanismsHow a fractionation table or a pulse-chase result tells one mechanism from another. Demanding; worth it for students who are writing the semester paper.Cell Biology · about 24 min · short film, simulation, 3D molecules, 5 self-test questions
