SOL Challenge Questions

Fifty-seven original practice questions for the Virginia Biology SOL, written for the concepts students find hardest. Tap an answer and you get the reasoning and where to study. Nothing is submitted.

Every question on this page is original, written by me and my colleagues for this page. They come from two kinds of experience. The first is ours: the concepts our students miss most often on our unit tests, in the wording that trips them up. The second is Virginia's: each year the Department of Education releases test items along with the share of students statewide who answered each one correctly, and some concepts come in under 40 percent across the whole state. Both go into this bank, and it grows as the year goes on.

Each question carries two ratings. A Schwebach challenge is a concept I want my own students to practice because I have watched them struggle with it, or because I know it will come back later in the year. A Virginia challenge is a concept students across the state found hard on a recent released item, with the statewide result quoted. Many questions are both.

Questions keep their numbers for good, so Q14 on a feedback slip or in a Canvas assignment always means the same question. The address #q14 jumps to one question, and ?q=3,14,22 shows just those. Your answers stay on this device until you clear them.

For teachers

Use any of these freely with a class. To assign a set, link to this page with the question numbers in the address, for example sol-challenge-questions.html?q=6,10,23, and students see only those. Print this set prints the questions showing, without answers. Every question names its SOL standard and the concept to study, so it works with any Virginia course, not only ours; the Canvas links are for Mr. Schwebach's own students and need a PWCS login.

New questions are added at the end with the next number. A question is never renumbered or reused; if one is retired, its number stays retired. Corrections and suggested questions: use the address on the home page.
