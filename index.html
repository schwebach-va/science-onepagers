If you teach, start here. Everything on this site is free to use, adapt, and hand to a class — and every source is in the open, so you can check any of it before you do.

Not a teacher? Start with the book and its argument — or the whole method on one sheet, Teaching #13.

I teach biology and chemistry, using these materials with my high school and introductory-college students. This site is the companion to Everybody’s Science, my in-progress book on evidence-based thinking.

Here is what you will find across this growing library:

  • Advanced Readings: Short, self-contained biology and chemistry assignments featuring key vocabulary and comprehension questions.
  • Teaching Resources: Classroom-facing guides, protocols, and reference materials.
  • Careers: A dedicated guidance section mapping out college trajectories and future opportunities.

New topics are added regularly. Once the book is published, the core articles will live here permanently. Science is an ever-evolving process under constant review. If you spot a factual error, please reach out via LinkedIn to let me know; I will update the content and log the correction under “About the Book.”
