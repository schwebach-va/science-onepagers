Chemistry Classroom Tools

The chemistry in a first-year biology course carries most of the weight and gets the least time. These are the activities I build for my dual-enrollment sections to close that gap, filed by the unit of NVCC BIO 101 they belong to, with the Campbell chapter and the calendar window for each. Every one runs in Safari on a school iPad, with no login and nothing to print. A tool keeps its Chem Tool number for good, and a tool that serves two units is listed under both.

Unit 2 Chemical Context of Life

Syllabus schedule · Quarter 1 · Aug 31–Sep 18 · 13 school days · 6–7 blocks per section

What this unit covers
  • Ch. 2 Chemical Context of Life
  • Due in this window Intro to Mastering Biology (4 pts)
  • Chem Tool #1 Bond Budget — carbon four, nitrogen three, oxygen two, hydrogen one, and seven molecules to build by spending every budget to zero Interactive · works on iPads · checks itself · about 20 minutes · Campbell Ch. 2 · leads into Ch. 3
  • Chem Tool #2 Chemistry Check — eighteen questions that find the exact spot where the chemistry stopped making sense, with a practice task for whatever they find Interactive · works on iPads · nothing submitted · about 15 minutes · six ideas, three questions each · take it before Unit 3 opens

Unit 3 Carbon and Molecular Diversity of Life

Syllabus schedule · Quarter 1 · Sep 21–Oct 6 · 11 school days · 5–6 blocks per section

What this unit covers
  • Ch. 3 Carbon and Molecular Diversity of Life
  • Due in this window Ch. 1 and 2 SAMs (12 pts)
  • Bio Tool #3 Carbon Check — seven ideas sorted out three questions at a time, with something to draw for each one that comes up short Interactive · works on iPads · nothing submitted · about 18 minutes · filed on the Biology side
  • Chem Tool #1 Bond Budget — the last three molecules hang a hydroxyl and a carbonyl off a carbon skeleton, which is where functional groups start Interactive · works on iPads · Campbell Ch. 3 · cross-listed from Unit 2
  • Chem Tool #3 Functional Groups in 3D — turn all seven groups in your hands, with the lone pairs sitting where VSEPR actually puts them and the phosphate carrying its two charges off to one side Interactive · works on iPads · drag to rotate · three drill modes · Campbell Ch. 3 · the phosphate is the one to sit with
