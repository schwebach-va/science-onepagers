Published Work of J. Reid Schwebach, PhD

Biology teacher at Battlefield High School and dual-enrollment instructor for Northern Virginia Community College. PhD in microbiology and immunology, Albert Einstein College of Medicine; EdM and MA, Teachers College, Columbia University; BS in biochemistry and BS in agricultural biology, New Mexico State University.

Microbiology, biomedical science and science education, newest first within each group. Wherever a free copy exists, the entry links straight to it. Where a publisher holds the full text behind a subscription, the entry says so and gives the best route to it.

Book

  • Schwebach, J. R. (2017). Cell Structure and Function: Mastering the Big Ideas. San Diego: Cognella Academic Publishing. 188 pp. ISBN 978-1-63487-903-3.

    Publisher accessBook listingFind in a library

    A workbook for university and advanced high school biology students on biochemistry and cell structure and function, with study questions, drawing pages, peer worksheets and downloadable pencasts. Twelve George Mason University learning assistants and undergraduate researchers contributed to it. Cognella holds the rights and sells it as a course text, so there is no open-access edition; instructors can request an examination copy from Cognella. A later edition is listed as Cell Structure and Function, ISBN 978-1-5165-5576-5.

Edited volumes

Two student research journals, twenty years apart. Students write each volume and are credited on it; Dr. Schwebach edits and mentors. Beacon Science came out of The Beacon School in New York City; Battlefield Science comes out of Battlefield High School, and Teaching #10 tells how it started.
