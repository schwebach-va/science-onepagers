This page isn’t here — and on this site, that matters.

Every article here carries a permanent number and a URL that never changes once it’s published, because Everybody’s Science cites this site in print, and a printed page can’t be corrected after it ships. A book pointing at a dead link is exactly the failure this site was built to prevent.

So if a link in the book, or anywhere else, brought you here: something is genuinely wrong, and I want to know about it. The last update and full history of every page are in the GitHub page history linked from the homepage footer — and the fix will be logged in the open, which is rather the whole point of the site.

Meanwhile, everything that exists is one click away: the full index lists every article by section and permanent number — Evidence, Biology, Chemistry, Teaching, and Careers — and the book page carries the companion links for every breakout box.

A wrong pointer, caught and corrected in the open, is the method working — not the method failing.
